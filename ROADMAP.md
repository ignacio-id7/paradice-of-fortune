# Roadmap

The work is organised in phases, and each phase in blocks. Every block ends with the test suite passing, the game playable from start to finish and the owner's review before the next one starts. A new world always goes through a mock-up with its palette and typography approved before its pieces are produced.

## Up next

### Android (in progress)
The full game on the owner's Android phone, played by touch in landscape, as a personal build installed by cable; it is not published in any store. Done: touch controls, the side buttons and a graphics preset of its own for the phone. Still to do: adjustments after the owner's test, the dice fairness test running on the phone itself, and the wrap-up.

### Online, 1 versus 1
A network technical test first, then online games between two computers: rooms, alternating turns, actions and dice trajectories in sync, disconnections, the turn timer and payouts.

### Three to four players
Against the AI and on the same computer first, then online. Planned once online 1 versus 1 is closed.

### Progression and endgame
- **The Book:** collection, chronicles, trophies, a record of every set of dice, travel stamps, titles and a map of the worlds; achievements, including secret ones.
- **Finishes** for the pieces, from the basic ones up to gold.
- **A prize machine** unlocked with the first pack, and a **wishing well** whose milestones unlock features: photo mode, more table layouts, a travelling merchant, a bigger table.
- **Travelling merchant** with rotating items.
- **Celebrations** for a Yacht and for the upper bonus in each world, with an option to reduce flashes.
- **Photo mode** with each world's camera filters.

## Version 1.0

Version 1.0 closes when the economy, multiplayer (AI, local and online) and the endgame are finished, together with the worlds produced by then: the Oak Inn, Frutiger Aero, the noir office and Fonda El Cacho.

## Worlds

Every world is a phase of its own: approved mock-up, pieces, a Yacht celebration, table objects, a scale of finishes and a pack.

- **Fifth world:** the gothic crypt (dark fantasy).
- **Confirmed:** a 1970s casino, space, a cyberpunk alley, a throne room, a crystal cave, a 1990s role-playing table, a steampunk workshop, a tea house among cherry blossoms, a paper world, and Christmas as a seasonal world.

## After 1.0

- Expansions with new worlds.
- Seasons: the fonda returns every September, and Halloween.
- AI opponents with personalities (bold, cautious).
- A roguelike mode with challenges and new rule sets, outside the records.
- A ranked online mode.
